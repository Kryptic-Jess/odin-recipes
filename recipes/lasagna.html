Lasagna Recipe

Rich, comforting layers of pasta, savoury sauce, and melted cheese

Lasagna is a timeless Italian classic that brings together layers of tender pasta sheets, rich meat sauce, creamy bechamel, and melted cheese. Baked until golden and bubbling, each layer creates a delicious balance of hearty flavours and comforting textures.

Best served fresh from the oven, this traditional dish is perfect for family dinners, special gatherings, or any occassion that calls for a satisfying homemade meal that was designed to be shared.
